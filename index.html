01

Who gets the CPU next.

Four schedulers pick the next process from the ready queue. Edit arrival and burst times, then compare the Gantt charts and waiting times they produce.

  • Team 01FCFS & SJF
  • Team 02Shortest Remaining Time First
  • Team 03Priority Scheduling
  • Team 04Round Robin

02

Grant nothing unsafe.

The Banker's algorithm checks every resource request against a safe sequence before it hands anything out, and shows the Allocation, Max and Need matrices as it goes.

  • Team 06Banker's Algorithm

03

Share without collisions.

Semaphores, a writer lock and five forks. Three classic problems show how processes share a resource without corrupting it or waiting on each other forever.

  • Team 07Producer-Consumer
  • Team 08Readers-Writers
  • Team 09Dining Philosophers

04

Move the head less.

Six disk schedulers work through a queue of cylinder requests. Each one draws the path of the read/write head and totals the cylinders it travels.

  • Team 10FCFS & SSTF
  • Team 11SCAN & C-SCAN
  • Team 12LOOK & C-LOOK

05

Fit it in. Fault less.

Place processes into free blocks with First, Best and Worst Fit. Then step through FIFO, LRU and Optimal page replacement one reference at a time.

  • Team 05First, Best & Worst Fit
  • Team 13FIFO & LRU
  • Team 14Optimal & comparison
